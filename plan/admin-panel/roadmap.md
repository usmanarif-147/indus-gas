# Admin Panel Roadmap

## Purpose

Give both partners one trusted place to manage the business and make decisions.

## MVP: include first

- Partner-only sign-in.
- Dashboard: active customers, today’s expenses, recent employee activity.
- Customer management: view, edit, change stage, add health tag.
- Review employee-submitted expenses.
- Manage employee accounts and assigned roles.
- Simple search and date filters.
- Activity history: who entered or changed a record and when.

## Next after MVP

- Delivery records and filled/empty cylinder counts.
- Customer balances, collections, and unpaid amounts.
- Cylinder stock view: warehouse, customer, and in transit.
- Purchases, supplier bills, fuel, maintenance, and other expense categories.
- Employee advances, salary deductions, and monthly settlement.
- Monthly profit/loss and cash-flow reports.

## Later improvements

- Charts for sales, collections, expenses, and customer health.
- Export to Excel/PDF.
- Approval process for large expenses or adjustments.
- Partner profit/loss split report.

## Not needed now

- Accounting-system integration.
- Tax filing automation.
- Route optimization.
- Complex multi-company support.

## Decisions still needed

- Which partner can edit or delete finalized records?
- Which expense amount requires partner approval?
- What reports must be available monthly?
