# Public Website Roadmap

## Purpose

Present Indus Gas professionally to prospective business customers and make contacting the company easy.

## MVP: include first

- Company introduction and business purpose.
- Services offered and the type of businesses served.
- Service-area information.
- Contact phone number, WhatsApp, email, and address.
- Simple contact or inquiry form.
- Mobile-friendly layout.
- Basic privacy/contact notice.

## Next after MVP

- Frequently asked questions.
- Product/cylinder information.
- Customer testimonials or trusted-client logos.
- Inquiry tracking in the admin panel.
- Urdu content if customers need it.

## Later improvements

- Search-engine visibility improvements.
- Service-area pages.
- Downloadable company profile.
- Careers page if the team grows.

## Not needed now

- Online ordering or online payments.
- Customer login portal.
- Live chat.
- Blog/news section.
- Full product catalogue or e-commerce store.

## Decisions still needed

- Final company name, logo, and colours.
- Official WhatsApp number and business email.
- Exact service areas and address.
- Whether the website should be in English only or English and Urdu.
