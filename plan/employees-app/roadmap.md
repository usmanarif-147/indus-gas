# Employees App Roadmap

## Purpose

Give employees a fast, phone-friendly way to enter operational data without WhatsApp messages.

## MVP: include first

- Employee sign-in; role is assigned by a partner, not selected freely.
- A role-based home screen.
- Salesman: add and update customer details.
- All employees: submit their own daily expenses.
- Expense details: amount, category, date, notes, and who entered it.
- Clear confirmation after every successful submission.
- Employees can view their own recent submissions.

## Role access in MVP

| Role | Main work |
|---|---|
| Salesman | Customers, customer status, own expenses |
| Driver | Own expenses, assigned delivery work when added |
| Helper | Own expenses, delivery support when added |

## Next after MVP

- Driver/helper delivery entry: filled cylinders delivered and empty cylinders collected.
- Customer delivery confirmation.
- Cash collection entry.
- View assigned deliveries and customer details.
- Photo proof for receipts or delivery confirmation.

## Later improvements

- Offline data entry and automatic sync when internet returns.
- Push reminders for assigned deliveries.
- Camera scanning or signature capture.
- Employee advance balance and salary-statement view.

## Not needed now

- Employee chat or WhatsApp replacement.
- Live GPS tracking.
- Customer-facing access in the employee app.
- Play Store publication; installed PWA is enough initially.

## Decisions still needed

- Which expense categories can each role submit?
- Can employees edit an expense after submission?
- Who confirms a delivery: driver, helper, customer, or all three?
